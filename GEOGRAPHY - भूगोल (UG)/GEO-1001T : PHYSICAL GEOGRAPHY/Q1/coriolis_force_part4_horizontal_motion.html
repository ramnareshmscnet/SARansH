Coriolis Force – Part 4

Effect on a Particle Moving Horizontally on Earth (2016 & 2019)

Questions

Q.2 (2016): What is Coriolis force? Explain its effect on a particle moving horizontally on the Earth's surface.

Q.12(a) (2019): Explain the effect of Coriolis force on a particle moving in the horizontal direction on Earth.

Theory (सिद्धान्त)

When a particle moves horizontally on the rotating Earth, it experiences a Coriolis force because Earth is a rotating frame of reference. This force is always perpendicular to the direction of motion and causes the path to bend.

$$\boxed{\vec F_C=-2m(\vec\omega\times\vec v)}$$
$$\boxed{F_C=2m\omega v\sin\theta}$$

हिन्दी: पृथ्वी पर क्षैतिज दिशा में गतिमान कण पर कोरिऑलिस बल कार्य करता है, जिससे उसका मार्ग सीधा न रहकर मुड़ जाता है।

Direction of Deflection

HemisphereEffect of Coriolis Force
Northern HemisphereParticle is deflected towards the right of its motion.
Southern HemisphereParticle is deflected towards the left of its motion.

Important Cases

MotionDirection of Deflection
NorthwardEast (Northern Hemisphere)
SouthwardWest (Northern Hemisphere)
EastwardEquatorward (small effect)
WestwardPoleward (small effect)

Illustration

In the Northern Hemisphere, a particle moving north is deflected toward the east (right-hand side).

Applications

  • Rotation of cyclones and anticyclones.
  • Ocean currents.
  • Long-range missiles and artillery.
  • Aircraft navigation.
  • Meteorology and weather forecasting.

Exam Tips

  • Always mention: Right in Northern Hemisphere, Left in Southern Hemisphere.
  • Draw a simple diagram to score better.
  • Write the vector equation and explain that the force is perpendicular to the motion.
